How it works (rules)
  1. Range: the first 30 minutes of the London session, 08:00–08:30 London time (14:00–14:30 Cambodia in summer, 15:00–15:30 in winter).
  2. Orders: Buy stop at the range high and Sell stop at the range low. The first one that fills cancels the other. If nothing fills by 15:00 London, delete both.
  3. Stop loss: the other side of the range. Do not move it to entry (tests showed that lowers results).
  4. Short-term: take profit at 1.5 × the range size; close anything still open at 23:00 Cambodia time.
  5. Long line: no fixed take profit; trailing stop 6 × ATR(15 min) that only moves in your favour; close after 24 hours or before the weekend.
  6. Auto (case by case): at the end of the range the desk checks the trend (price above or below the 15 min EMA 200). The order that goes WITH the trend gets no TP and a trailing stop (Long line). The order that goes AGAINST the trend gets the 1.5 × TP and closes the same day (Short-term).
  7. One trade per day. Size the lot so the stop loss costs at most your risk % (the desk shows the lot).
